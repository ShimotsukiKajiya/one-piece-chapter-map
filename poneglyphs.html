🪨 Poneglyphs

The indestructible cubes of ancient text scattered across the world. Four Road Poneglyphs that triangulate to Laugh Tale; the Rio Poneglyph that holds the truth of the Void Century; informational stones at Alabasta, Skypiea, Fish-Man Island. Robin is one of the few alive who can read them.
