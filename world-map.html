Artur's Interactive One Piece World Map

After five years of research from Oda's own published sketches, Artur (Library of Ohara) shipped the authoritative interactive map in May 2026 — every canon location, every sea, every hidden detail Oda has ever drawn. The Codex points you there rather than duplicating five years of his careful work.
