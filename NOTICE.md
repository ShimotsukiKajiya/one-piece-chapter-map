## Why this licensing structure

**MIT for code, CC BY-NC for editorial content.**

The split lets the *technical scaffolding* (HTML / JS / build pipeline)
be useful to anyone who wants to build their own fan reference for
their own series — that's good for the fan-tooling community and costs
nothing.

The *editorial work* (categorizations, severity rankings, design
documents, taxonomies, the SpoilerGuard architecture) is the
intellectual contribution that takes meaningful time and judgment.
Protecting that under CC BY-NC means it can still be shared for
fan/educational purposes with credit, but can't be lifted into a
commercial fan-product clone without negotiation.

The branding (Shimotsuki Codex, SpoilerGuard) is reserved as a marker
of the original — others can build similar things, but can't claim to
*be* this project.
