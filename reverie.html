⏰ Reverie & World Events

The Reverie (Levely) is the World Council — every four years, the kings and queens of 50 member nations gather at Mariejois. The most lore-shaking political events of canon happen here. Imu was first revealed at the Reverie. Cobra died there. The Mother Flame was used post-Reverie to erase Lulusia. Below: every major Reverie + post-Reverie political event in chronological order.
