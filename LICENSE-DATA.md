## What's covered here

- Theory analyses, tier classifications (canon / likely / speculation /
  rumour / disproven), and intent tagging (serious / jocular /
  confirmation / dodged)

## Why this split?

The MIT licence on the code lets anyone fork the site framework freely —
that's the technical scaffolding, and sharing it benefits everyone.

CC BY-NC on the curated content protects the *editorial work* — the
tier judgments, structural compilations, and hand-written notes — from
being lifted into commercial fan-product sites without credit or
compensation, while still allowing forks for personal use, study,
translation, or non-commercial fan projects.
