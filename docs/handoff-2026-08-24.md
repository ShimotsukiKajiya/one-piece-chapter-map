## Non-negotiable rules

1. **FAIL-LATE.** Torn between two chapters, take the LATER one. Over-hiding
   costs a little completeness; leaking costs the reader the story.
2. **Never inflate a tier.** 🟢 CANON = Oda direct (manga panel, SBS, Vivre
   Card). 🔵 LIKELY = wiki + corroboration. 🟣 SPECULATION = wiki only.
3. **Report, never auto-promote.** Nothing may change a fact's tier without the
   maintainer. Findings go to `docs/` and `curate.html`.
4. **Uncertainty goes in `_qa_flags`, never in reader-facing text.** Thirteen
   `(QA: chapter approximate)` strings once shipped to production inside notes.
5. **Every chapter number you assert carries a marker**: ✓ certain, ~ confident,
   ? needs QA. Anything `?` also goes to `_qa_flags`.
6. **Derive from the data, never from recall.** A gap in memory becomes a
   permanent gap in the page. This is how Clou D. Clover went missing.

## Traps already paid for — do not rediscover these

- **`derive → bake.py → verify`.** The lore JSONs are baked into the HTML.
  Deriving without re-baking leaves pages serving stale data.
- **`character.html` runs a synchronous first render ~line 470**, far above its
  helper block. A `let`/`const` declared with the helpers is still in its
  temporal dead zone and the page sticks on "Loading…". Declare shared constants
  at the top. Function declarations are fine.
- **`innerText` across an iframe boundary degrades to `textContent`** and reads
  hidden `<script type="application/json">` blocks. Any browser sweep must strip
  script/style before measuring, and must confirm the page actually rendered —
  a clean result on an unrendered page looks identical to success.
- **A rung must override EVERY leaking field**, not just the main text. Fields it
  does not name fall through from the base entry, which carries the latest
  wording.
- **`resolveRungs()` in `lore-gate.js` and `resolve_rungs()` in
  `scripts/lib/gate.py` must change together.**
- **Cache-bust when you change a shipped asset** (`?v=N` in every HTML file).
  `settings.js` was unversioned, so a fix in it could never reach a reader.
- **Deriving a technique's chapter from its USER's debut is wrong.** Ifrit Jambe
  (Wano) inherited Sanji's Ch. 43.
